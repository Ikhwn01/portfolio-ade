# DESIGN.md — Direction: Executive Professional (Portfolio Ade)

## 1. Identity & Concept
- **Product**: Personal Engineering Portfolio for Ade
- **Target Audience**: Recruiters, Tech Leads, Corporate Clients, and Engineering Managers looking for a dependable Fullstack / Software Engineer
- **Core Archetype**: Executive Pro (Option 4) — structured, credible, authoritative, and clean
- **Tone & Mood**: Professional, confident, high clarity, trustworthy

## 2. Color Palette & Theming
- **Theme**: Crisp White & Professional Slate/Navy
- **Surface**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) as primary canvas, with subtle Off-White / Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) for section alternating and cards
- **Borders**: Defined, crisp Slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`) borders for clear visual boundaries
- **Text**: Deep Slate-900 (`#0F172A`) for headlines (high contrast WCAG AAA), Slate-600 (`#475569`) for readable body copy
- **Accent**: Executive Navy / Indigo (`#1E3A8A` / `#2563EB`) for focused CTAs, active states, and badge highlights
- **Success / Status**: Emerald-600 (`#059669`) for positive indicators and form success feedback

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter — clean, modern, legible, professional weight hierarchy (Bold 700 / SemiBold 600)
- **Body & Captions**: Inter / system sans-serif — 15px/16px for body with 1.6 line height for comfortable readability
- **Mono / Tech Tags**: JetBrains Mono / SF Mono for tech stacks and version numbers

## 4. Layout & Structure
- **Container**: Max width `1152px` (`max-w-6xl`), centered with generous gutter padding
- **Navigation**: Sticky top navigation bar with clean branding ("Ade .dev"), semantic jump links (`#about`, `#skills`, `#projects`, `#experience`, `#contact`), and direct action CTA
- **Hero**: Executive layout with avatar/monogram, professional summary, quick contact badges, and primary actions (Contact Me, View Projects, Download CV)
- **Sections**:
  1. Header / Navbar with mobile sheet menu
  2. Hero (Executive Overview)
  3. Competencies & Tech Stack (Categorized: Frontend, Backend, Tools/Architecture)
  4. Featured Projects (Interactive tabs/filters, detailed case-study cards)
  5. Professional Experience & Education Timeline
  6. Contact Section (Direct contact info + Functional React form posting to Node.js backend)
  7. Footer with copyright and navigation links

## 5. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Controlled, confident, no frantic animations or neon bursts)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Structured grid hierarchy with clear visual balance)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Subtle hover micro-interactions, smooth accordion/mobile toggle, polite form feedback transitions)
